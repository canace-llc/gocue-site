Frequently asked questions

How does GoCue know when to alert me?

GoCue uses real-time Google Maps traffic data to calculate how long your trip will take. It works backward from your arrival time, accounting for current traffic conditions, to determine the latest moment you need to leave — then alerts you at your chosen notice time.

How accurate are the departure times?

GoCue uses the same traffic data that powers Google Maps, updated in real time. The closer your departure time, the more accurate the estimate, since it incorporates live traffic conditions as they develop.

Can I set alerts for future dates?

Yes. You can schedule alerts days or weeks in advance. For future trips, GoCue uses historical traffic patterns for that time of day and day of week, then switches to live traffic data as your departure time approaches.

Does GoCue work internationally?

Yes. GoCue works anywhere Google Maps has traffic data, including the United States, Mexico, Canada, and most major cities worldwide.

Why do I need an account?

An account lets GoCue save your alerts and recent destinations across devices and app reinstalls. You can sign in with Apple, Google, or an email address and password.

I signed in with Apple (or Google) before. Can I switch sign-in methods?

Each sign-in method creates a separate account. If you originally signed in with Apple or Google, continue using that method to access your saved alerts. If you need help recovering an account, email us at support@canacellc.com.

How do I cancel an alert?

Go to My Alerts, swipe left on any alert, and tap Delete.

How much does GoCue cost?

GoCue is free for up to 5 alerts per month. For unlimited alerts, upgrade to GoCue Pro. Pricing varies by region and is shown in the app before you subscribe.

What counts as one alert?

Each time you tap "Set Alert" counts as one alert toward your monthly limit, regardless of whether you receive the notification or delete the alert early. The free monthly counter resets on the first day of each calendar month.

How do I manage or cancel my GoCue Pro subscription?

GoCue Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple Account. To manage or cancel it, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, select GoCue Pro, and choose your option. Cancelling stops the automatic renewal; you keep Pro access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do refunds work?

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests are handled by Apple rather than by us. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you have questions, email us and we'll help where we can.

Will GoCue always get me there on time?

GoCue provides departure recommendations based on real-time and historical Google Maps traffic data, but it cannot guarantee on-time arrival. Traffic conditions, accidents, road closures, and other factors can change rapidly and unpredictably. Always use your own judgment, leave extra time for important appointments, and never rely on GoCue as your sole planning tool for time-critical travel such as flights, medical appointments, or legal proceedings.

I have a feature request or found a bug. How do I tell you?

We'd love to hear from you. You can use the in-app feedback option, or email us directly at support@canacellc.com.
